# House rules

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are set out in the house rules: at night from 10 pm to 6 am and at midday from 1 pm to 3 pm. During these times, noisy work and loud music are not allowed.

## Stairwell and shared areas
The stairwell, corridors and escape routes must be kept clear. Bicycles, prams and shoe racks must not be left there permanently.

## Pets
Small animals such as hamsters or fish can be kept without permission. Dogs and cats require the written consent of the property management.

## Balconies and barbecues
Charcoal barbecues are not allowed on balconies. Electric grills are permitted as long as other residents are not disturbed.
