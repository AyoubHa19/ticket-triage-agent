# Keys

## Lost keys
If you lose a key to your flat, please inform the property management in writing. With a master key system the lock cylinder may have to be replaced; who pays depends on the cause and on your tenancy agreement. Replacement keys are only issued for a fee and against a signature.

## Additional keys
Additional keys, for example for a new flatmate, can only be cut with the written consent of the property management. For master key systems you must not hire a locksmith.

## Mailbox key
A replacement mailbox key can be requested from the property management. Please state your flat number.
