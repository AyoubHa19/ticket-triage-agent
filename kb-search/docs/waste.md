# Waste and recycling

## Separating waste
General waste, paper, packaging and organic waste are collected separately. The bins are in the bin room on the ground floor. Please do not leave items next to the bins.

## Bulky waste
Bulky waste must not be left in the basement, the stairwell or the yard. Collection must be booked with the council's waste service, which will give you a collection date. Put the items out on the pavement only the evening before.

## Collection days
The collection days for general waste, paper and organic waste are posted in the bin room and listed in the council's waste calendar.
