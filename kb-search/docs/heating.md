# Heating

## Maintenance appointments
The annual heating service is announced at least two weeks in advance on a notice in the stairwell. Please give the technician access to your flat. If you cannot keep the appointment, let us know at least three days beforehand so that a new date can be arranged.

## Radiator not getting warm
If a radiator does not get properly warm, first bleed it with a radiator key. Also check that the thermostatic valve is turned up far enough. If that does not help, report the fault through the portal or by email, stating the room and the radiator.

## Heating failure in winter
If the whole heating system fails, it is an urgent case. During office hours call the property management; outside office hours call the emergency service on the number posted in the stairwell.
