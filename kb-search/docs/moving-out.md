# Moving out and handover

## Ending your tenancy
Notice must be given in writing. The notice period is set out in your tenancy agreement. The property management confirms receipt of your notice in writing.

## Handover
The property management arranges an appointment for the handover. The flat must be handed over swept clean. A handover report is drawn up that records the condition of the flat and the meter readings. All keys must be returned.

## Deposit
The deposit is repaid after the handover, once any outstanding claims have been checked. The check can take until the service charge statement for the last year is available.

## Forwarding address
Please send your new address to the property management in writing and set up mail forwarding with the post office.
