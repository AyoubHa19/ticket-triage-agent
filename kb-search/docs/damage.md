# Reporting damage

## How to report damage
Report damage through the portal or by email with a description, where it is in the flat and, if possible, a photo. Please report urgent damage by phone.

## Water damage
In case of water damage, turn off the main stopcock if you can and switch off the affected circuits. Inform the property management or the emergency service immediately. Take photos of the damage for the insurance.

## Mould
Mould is often caused by too little ventilation. Air the rooms thoroughly several times a day and move furniture slightly away from the outside walls. Please still report mould in writing with photos; a technician will check the cause.

## Your flat or the common parts
Who is responsible for a repair depends on whether it affects your own flat or the common parts of the building (roof, facade, pipes inside the walls). The property management clarifies who is responsible.
