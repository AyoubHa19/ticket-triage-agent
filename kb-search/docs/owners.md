# Information for owners

## Owners' meeting
The invitation to the owners' meeting is usually sent in writing at least three weeks before the date. The minutes are sent to all owners after the meeting.

## Annual service charge accounts
The service charge accounts for owners are prepared every year. A copy for your tax return can be downloaded from the owner portal at any time or requested from the property management.

## Register of resolutions
All resolutions passed are recorded in the register of resolutions. Owners can inspect the register by appointment.
