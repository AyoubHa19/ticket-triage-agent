# Service charge statement

## When the statement is sent
The service charge statement is sent no later than twelve months after the end of the billing period. Tenants usually receive it during the second quarter of the following year.

## Inspecting the receipts
On request, tenants can inspect the receipts behind the statement at the property management office by appointment. Copies of the receipts are available for a small fee.

## Additional payments and credits
Additional payments must be transferred within the deadline stated in the statement. Credits are offset against the next advance payment or transferred on request. Payment in instalments can be requested in writing.

## Objections to the statement
Objections to the statement must be made in writing and are reviewed by a member of staff.
